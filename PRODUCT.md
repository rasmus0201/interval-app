# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Stack

Native SwiftUI with Swift 6, using Apple frameworks only. The bundle identifier uses the `com.bundsgaard` namespace. The project must not use a Medware development team or signing profile.

## Users

People who run interval workouts and need to read and control a timer while moving.

## Product Purpose

Interval makes a workout timer quick to configure and easy to follow at a glance. Success means a person can start a useful session in seconds, keep the phone locked in a pocket, and still understand phase changes through sound and vibration.

## Positioning

One focused timer screen carries the full workout. Setup stays compact, while the active session gives time and phase the strongest visual priority. Previous workouts act as reusable presets without requiring a separate preset system.

## Operating Context

The user holds, props up, or keeps an iPhone locked in a pocket during a workout. The app must remain legible at arm's length and provide distinct sound and haptic phase cues.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Configure work duration in minutes and seconds.
- Configure rest duration.
- Configure repetitions per round and number of rounds.
- Optionally configure a pause between rounds.
- Start, pause, resume, skip, and reset a workout.
- Configure a global start countdown of 0, 10, 30, or 60 seconds.
- Configure global workout feedback as silent, vibration, or tones.
- Play distinct countdown tones when work starts, work ends, and rest ends.
- Provide a short vibration or sound cue during the workout where useful.
- Show the current phase, countdown, repetition, round, and progress.
- Keep timing and audible cues working while the screen is locked.
- Save completed workout history on the device.
- Load a previous workout configuration from history.
- Save the most recent configuration and global settings on the device.
- Work without an account or network connection.
- Support iPhone in the first version.
- Use native iOS controls and conventions.

## Evidence on Hand

No brand assets, customer evidence, or visual references were provided. The product name is Interval. Future work must not invent commercial claims.

## Product Principles

- Make the next action obvious.
- Keep active timing legible at a distance.
- Give clear phase-change feedback through more than one sense.
- Preserve the user's setup and workout history without adding account work.
- Follow native iOS behavior and accessibility settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, sufficient contrast, Reduce Motion, and cues that do not depend on color alone.
