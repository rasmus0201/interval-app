Support

Let's get you back in rhythm.

Sound on the Lock Screen

Check your phone's media volume and the app's volume under Settings. Turn on Beeps, on their own or together with Vibration. The app can play its signals even when the Silent switch is on.

Live Activity doesn't appear

Open the iOS Settings for Kyclaro and make sure Live Activities are allowed. Then start a new workout.

History

Only finished workouts are saved. Open the History tab to load a previous setup or delete a workout.

Contact

Open a support issue on GitHub. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.
