Privacy Policy

Your workout data stays on your iPhone.

Last updated October 5, 2026

Data collection

Kyclaro does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. The app uses no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

Local storage

The app stores your settings, your most recent setup and the history of finished workouts locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app's features.

Deletion

You can delete individual workouts from the history in the app. When you delete the app, iOS also deletes its local data.

Network and accounts

The app does not require an account or an internet connection. It does not send workout data to any server.

Contact

You can reach us by opening a support issue on GitHub.
