# Roadmap

These steps follow the first App Store release. None of them is implemented yet, and version 1.0 contains no StoreKit, HealthKit, or watchOS code.

## Step 3 – Kyclaro Pro

A one-time purchase through StoreKit 2. The core timer stays free and fully usable, with no subscriptions or ads.

- Save and name workout programs.
- More reusable presets.
- Composite workouts made of several blocks, such as warm-up, intervals, and cool-down in one program.
- EMOM and AMRAP.
- Possibly presets for boxing and other interval training.

## Step 4 – Apple Health

A free HealthKit integration.

- Save finished sessions as workouts only after the person grants permission.
- Use a fitting workout type and duration, and avoid duplicate entries.
- Keep the app fully usable without HealthKit permission, and request as little data as possible.

## Step 5 – Apple Watch

A standalone watchOS timer, not only a remote control for the iPhone.

- Start and finish a workout on the watch, with a clear countdown and haptic phase changes.
- Show the current round, repetition, and interval, plus heart rate.
- Use HealthKit workout sessions.
- Sync programs and results with the iPhone, and work without the phone nearby.

The pricing model for the watch app is still open.
